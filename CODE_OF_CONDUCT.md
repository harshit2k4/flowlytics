# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Be helpful. Focus on the work.

Flowlytics is a health app built for real people, many of whom are navigating sensitive personal topics. That context matters. Everyone contributing to or engaging with this project (through issues, pull requests, discussions, or the wiki) is expected to treat others with basic decency.

**A note on the nature of this project:** Flowlytics uses mathematical modelling to estimate cycle patterns. It is not a medical product. Discussions in this project space should reflect that. Do not give or encourage others to follow specific medical advice based on the app's output. If someone raises a health concern in an issue or discussion, point them to a healthcare professional.

---

## What is expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to the domain
- Give feedback on the work, not the person
- Accept that there are many valid approaches to a problem
- Acknowledge when you are wrong or do not know something

---

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Dismissing contributions based on who someone is rather than what they wrote
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent
- Sexualised language or imagery in any project space

This project specifically invites contributions from women developers and health researchers. Behaviour that makes that group feel unwelcome is taken especially seriously.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this code of conduct, open a private issue or contact the maintainer directly via GitHub.

Reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion. We will not publicly name people without their consent unless the situation makes it unavoidable.

---

## Enforcement

The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously violate these expectations. The goal is not to punish; it is to keep the project a place where good work can happen.

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, wiki, and any other forum associated with Flowlytics.
